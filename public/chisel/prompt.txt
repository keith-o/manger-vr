Early evening dusk, twilight and oil lamps: Interior of a first-century Judean village house in Bethlehem, lower animal-stabling level. A rough-hewn carved limestone manger filled with golden straw stands on a straw-strewn packed-earth floor. Rough limestone and cracked mud-plaster walls merge into a dark rock-cut cave wall, with worn plastered stone steps rising to a raised family platform holding large terracotta storage jars, woven baskets, and rolled reed mats under a low ceiling of wooden beams and reed matting. Warm golden clay oil lamps in wall niches blend with faint cool indigo dusk light. Cozy, intimate, ancient, photorealistic, no people, no animals.
